Guides

SBA loans, commercial debt, and mortgage financing come with dense paperwork, shifting rules, and jargon that changes meaning depending on who you ask. These guides strip away the fluff and give you the factual, step-by-step information you need to prepare, apply, and close with confidence. Every guide is written for people who are doing the work themselves, not for people who are shopping for a broker. None of these pages can approve, quote, or guarantee a loan — but they can help you ask the right questions and avoid the most common delays.

How to use these guides

Start with the guide that matches your immediate need. If you are preparing to apply, begin with the 7(a) playbook and keep the document guide open in a second tab. If you are deciding between 7(a) and 504, read the 504 overview and compare the two programs side by side.

Each guide stands on its own, but they cross-reference each other at the points where topics overlap. When a guide links to another, it opens a specific section, not the top of the page, so you can keep your place and follow the chain.

These guides are living documents. SBA rules and fee schedules change, and we update each page when they do. The "Updated" date on every page tells you when the last substantive change was made.
