Trust and transparency

Editorial policy and review standards

LoansFlow publishes educational loan guides and calculator tools. Because financing decisions can affect households and businesses, we aim to be clear about where our information comes from, how calculations work, what we can and cannot do, and how readers can report errors.

Important: LoansFlow is an independent educational site, not a lender, broker, bank, credit counselor, or law/tax/financial advisory firm. Content and calculators are for general information only and are not offers, approvals, quotes, or professional advice.

What we publish

  • Loan-type explainers covering common SBA, commercial, equipment, and mortgage financing options.
  • Step-by-step guides on documents, eligibility concepts, lender comparisons, and application preparation.
  • Calculators and estimators that show payments, fees, affordability ranges, DSCR, refinance break-even points, and related planning figures.
  • Reference tools such as directories, grant search interfaces, and news/rate summaries sourced from public data or original publishers.

How we research and source information

When a page makes a factual claim about a program, form, fee, limit, or process, we prefer primary sources. Depending on the topic, that may include SBA.gov program pages and notices, CFPB guidance, IRS publications, Federal Reserve data, Freddie Mac survey releases, Grants.gov, HUD resources, or the lender/program materials being described.

  • We link to sources when practical so readers can verify current terms.
  • We distinguish program rules from general guidance and from calculator estimates.
  • We avoid presenting examples as guarantees. Sample numbers are illustrative unless clearly labeled otherwise.
  • For topics that change frequently (rates, fee schedules, deadlines, eligibility details), we encourage readers to confirm current terms with the lender or issuing agency before acting.

Calculator methodology

Our calculators use standard amortization math and published fee/rule logic where applicable. The formulas, assumptions, and data sources are documented on our How Our Numbers Work page. Calculator outputs are estimates only. Actual lender pricing depends on credit profile, collateral, documentation, underwriting, market conditions, fees, and product terms.

Review and updates

We review content for clarity, broken links, outdated references, and material changes to commonly referenced programs. Pages may display an updated or last-reviewed date when substantive changes are made. Because external programs and rates change outside our control, a page can become incomplete between reviews; readers should verify time-sensitive details with primary sources.

Independence and monetization

LoansFlow is independently operated. We may display advertising (including Google AdSense) and may include clearly labeled partner or sponsored links where applicable. Advertising or partner relationships do not determine calculator math, and we do not accept payment to present inaccurate information as fact. If a listing or link is sponsored, informational only, or not an endorsement, we aim to label it clearly.

Corrections and feedback

If you find an error, outdated figure, broken source, or unclear explanation, please report it through our contact page. Include the page URL, the issue you found, and the correct source if available. We prioritize factual corrections related to program rules, calculator behavior, fees, eligibility descriptions, and source links.

Who this site is for

LoansFlow is designed for borrowers, small business owners, and homebuyers who want a plain-English starting point before speaking with a lender, SBA resource partner, housing counselor, accountant, or attorney. It is not a substitute for individualized professional advice.
