Why this matters when you borrow

Rates on mortgages follow long-term Treasury yields. Rates on many business loans and lines of credit follow the prime rate, which moves with Federal Reserve decisions. Rules from the CFPB change what lenders must disclose. Reading the news will not time the market for you, but it helps you understand why a quote changed.
