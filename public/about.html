About LoansFlow

LoansFlow is a free calculator suite for small business and home financing.

Core tools cover SBA 7(a), SBA 504, and conventional commercial loans — DSCR, guaranty fees, balloons, and amortization — plus mortgage payment, refinance, and affordability calculators.

Everything runs in your browser. We do not collect loan applications, sell leads, or require an account.
