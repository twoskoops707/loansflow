About LoansFlow

LoansFlow is a free, independent calculator and resource site for small business and home financing. We built it for borrowers who want to understand the numbers before they talk to a lender — and for the guides, directories, and checklists that make the process less opaque.

What we offer

Calculators. Nine tools covering SBA 7(a) and 504 estimates, commercial amortization, lines of credit, DSCR, mortgage payments (PITI), payment breakdowns, amortization ledgers, refinance comparisons, affordability, and a rates-and-news dashboard. Everything runs in your browser — no account, no application, no lead form.

Guides. Step-by-step instructions for the parts calculators cannot do: the SBA 7(a) playbook, the SBA 504 program, loan documents by loan type, an interactive checklist with downloadable versions, how to choose a lender, a loan glossary, and help for every tool.

Directory. A searchable national directory of all 67 SBA district offices, SBA national contacts, resource partners (SCORE, SBDCs, Women's Business Centers, Veterans Business Outreach Centers), and 18 loan marketplaces and brokerages — with addresses, phone numbers, hours, and verification notes.

Editorial standards

Program facts — loan maximums, guarantee percentages, rates, fees, SBA forms, and office contact details — are verified against primary sources such as SBA.gov and reviewed on a regular cadence. Pages carry an "Updated" date so you can tell how current they are. We write everything ourselves, link to the sources we rely on, and label marketplace listings as informational rather than endorsements.

Numbers produced by the calculators are estimates for informational purposes only. They are not offers, approvals, or guarantees of credit, and they are not financial, tax, or legal advice.

What LoansFlow is not

We are not a lender, broker, or financial advisor. We do not take applications, collect personal financial data, or sell leads. Your calculations never leave your browser.
