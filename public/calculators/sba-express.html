SBA Express loan calculator

Model an SBA Express loan, which is capped at $500,000, with its payment, fee and total interest.

This calculator needs JavaScript. Turn it on to run the numbers; nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Updated November 2, 2026. Math reviewed against our methodology.

How to use it

This version starts at a $150,000 loan with the 50% Express guarantee. Change the amount, rate and term to match the offer you are considering and compare the payment with the standard 7(a) calculator.

How it is calculated

Same math as the SBA 7(a) calculator: rate = base + spread, payment from the amortization formula, upfront fee on the guaranteed portion. The difference is the lower guarantee (50% for Express) and a $500,000 maximum loan size.

Worked example

A $150,000 Express loan at 10.00% for 10 years has a payment of $1,982.26 and $87,871 in total interest. The guaranteed portion is $75,000, and the 2% upfront fee on loans of $150,000 or less is $1,500. These are the calculator’s starting values, so you can reproduce them above.

Common questions

Why would a lender offer Express?

Express loans trade a smaller guarantee for a faster lender-driven process, so they suit smaller amounts and faster decisions.

Is a line of credit possible?

Yes. Express can be a term loan or a line of credit. This calculator models a term loan.

About this calculator

This tool runs entirely in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere. Results are estimates, not offers or quotes, and your lender’s numbers will differ. See how our numbers work for the formulas and assumptions.
