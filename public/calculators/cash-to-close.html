How to use it

Enter the total project cost and your down payment percentage, choose whether the SBA guarantee fee is paid in cash or financed into the loan, then enter your expected lender fee and third-party costs. The estimate adds your down payment, the guarantee fee (if paid in cash), lender and packaging fees, appraisal and environmental reports, legal and title, prepaid insurance and a working capital reserve.

How it is calculated

The loan is the project cost minus your down payment. The guarantee fee is a percentage of the SBA-guaranteed portion: 2% on loans up to $150,000, 3% from $150,001 to $700,000, and above $700,000 3.5% of the guaranteed portion up to $1 million plus 3.75% of any guaranteed amount over $1 million (FY 2027). The lender fee is a percentage of the loan. All other lines are the amounts you enter, because they vary by lender, property and state.

Worked example

On a $700,000 project with 10% down, you put down $70,000 and borrow $630,000. The SBA guarantees 75%, or $472,500, so the fee at 3% is $14,175. With a 1% lender fee ($6,300), $8,000 of reports, $6,000 of legal and title, $4,000 of prepaid items and a $25,000 reserve, the estimate is about $133,475 of cash to close. Those are the calculator’s starting values, so you can reproduce them above.

Common questions

Is the down payment the only cash I need at closing?

No. Fees, third-party reports, legal costs, prepaid items and any reserve your lender requires are usually additional.

Who pays the SBA guarantee fee?

The lender pays it to the SBA and normally passes it to the borrower, either in cash at closing or financed into the loan.
