Where the numbers come from

All figures come from the U.S. Small Business Administration’s public 7(a) & 504 FOIA loan data (publisher: SBA; license: U.S. Government Works, public domain). The files used here are dated September 30, 2026. SBA updates the data quarterly, typically about a month after each quarter ends, and these pages are rebuilt from the newest file when it appears. Dollar amounts are nominal (not adjusted for inflation).

  • Loan: Any 7(a) approval record in the file, including loans later cancelled (CANCLD), matching how SBA reports approvals. Cancelled counts are shown separately.
  • Dollars: GrossApproval, the full approved amount in nominal dollars (not adjusted for inflation).
  • Fiscal year: Federal fiscal year, October 1 through September 30, as recorded in ApprovalFY.
  • State: ProjectState, the state where the financed project is located.
  • Industry: First two digits of the NAICS code on the loan record, grouped into NAICS sectors.
  • Medians are shown only for groups with at least 20 loans.

The summary behind these pages is published as one JSON file with no borrower names or individual loan records. Approvals are not a forecast of whether a particular application will be approved, and they are not rate quotes.
