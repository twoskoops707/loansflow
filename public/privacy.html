Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

LoansFlow (“we”, “our”, or “us”) operates https://loansflow.loan/ (the “Site”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we and our service providers collect when you use the Site, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Calculator Usage: All loan calculations run entirely in your web browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit the loan amounts, interest rates, or other financial data you enter into our calculators.

Automatically Collected Information: When you visit the Site, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) and our advertising and analytics providers may automatically collect information about your device and usage, such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, and the dates and times of your visits, through standard server logs and similar technologies.

Cookies: We and our advertising partners use cookies and similar technologies. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help analyze web traffic, remember preferences and serve ads. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

Contact form: If you contact us through the contact form, we receive the name, email address, optional phone number and message you submit. We use them only to respond to you, and we do not sell them.

Email updates: If you subscribe to our monthly rate update, we receive your email address and send it to our email service provider, which delivers the update. We use it only for that purpose, and every message includes a way to unsubscribe.

Grants search: The grants search sends the keywords and filters you choose to the public Grants.gov service so it can return results. Nothing else is sent.

Bank search: If you use the bank search in our directory, the state and city you enter are sent to the FDIC’s public BankFind service to return results. The rates shown on the Site are loaded from our own server.

Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on the Site. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Site and other sites on the Internet.

Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google’s Ads Settings or youradchoices.com/control.

Fonts, Embeds and Affiliate Links

The Site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests them from Google and your IP address is visible to Google. Some pages may include optional third-party embeds (for example, social media posts), which may set their own cookies. Some pages contain affiliate or sponsored links; when you click one, you leave the Site and the destination company’s own privacy policy applies. See our advertising and affiliate disclosure.

How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Site
  • Understand and improve how the Site is used
  • Respond to messages and send the updates you request
  • Display advertisements through Google AdSense

Your Choices and California Privacy Rights

You can opt out of personalized advertising using the links above and manage cookies in your browser. California residents may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about them and to request deletion; to make a request, contact us. We do not sell the information you send us through the contact form or email sign-up.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to help protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s Privacy

The Site is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time by posting the new version on this page and updating the “Last Updated” date.

Contact Us
