SBA Express Loan

A faster, lender-processed SBA loan up to $500,000, including lines of credit.

Short answer: SBA Express is a faster version of the 7(a) with a 50% SBA guarantee, for amounts up to $500,000. Unlike the standard 7(a), it can be a revolving line of credit as well as a term loan.

What it is

SBA Express is a version of the 7(a) built for speed. Approved lenders use much of their own process instead of the full SBA package, and in exchange the SBA guarantees less (50%). It can be a term loan or a revolving line of credit, which the standard 7(a) cannot be.

Because the lender carries more risk, Express works best when you already have a banking relationship and a solid file.

A smaller guarantee is the trade for the lender using its own, faster process.

A good fit if you want

  • Amounts up to $500,000 where speed matters
  • A revolving SBA-backed line of credit
  • Borrowers with an existing relationship at an Express lender

Probably not the right tool if

  • Loans larger than $500,000
  • Weak collateral or cash flow, since the guarantee is only 50%

Typical requirements

  • Same core eligibility as the 7(a): for-profit, size standard, owner equity, credit-elsewhere test
  • Personal guarantees from 20% owners
  • Lender must hold Express authority

How to apply

  1. Ask your bankNot every SBA lender offers Express. Ask whether yours has Express authority.
  2. Prepare the fileExpect the lender's own application plus SBA Form 1919, financial statements and tax returns.
  3. Underwrite and approveThe lender decides, without waiting for SBA review.
  4. Close and fundOnce approved, closing moves like a conventional loan.

Need a lender, an SBA district office or a free counselor? Use the national directory.

Common questions

How is Express different from a regular 7(a)?

Lower guarantee (50%), a $500,000 maximum, and the lender uses its own procedures instead of the standard SBA ones.

Do veterans get fee relief?

For FY 2027, SBA Express loans to businesses owned and controlled by a veteran or the spouse of a veteran carry a $0 upfront fee.
