Contact us

Questions, corrections, or feedback about a calculation? Write us a message and we'll get back to you.

What we can help with

LoansFlow is an educational site. Write to us to report an error in a figure or a calculator, to suggest a guide or calculator you would like to see, to ask how one of our calculators works, or to tell us about a link that no longer works. Corrections matter to us: every figure on this site carries a date and a named source, and pages are updated when a source changes. See our editorial policy for how we check and date our facts.

What to include

  • The address of the page and what looks wrong, with the figure you expected and your source if you have one.
  • For a calculator, the numbers you entered and the result you saw.
  • For a suggestion, the situation you are trying to solve, so we can write something useful.

What we can’t do

We are not a lender, a broker or a financial advisor. We can’t review a loan application, tell you whether a lender will approve you, give personal tax, legal or investment advice, or recommend a particular lender. For a specific loan, contact a lender directly, a local SBA resource partner (see the phone directory) or a licensed professional.

Please don’t send sensitive information

Do not include Social Security numbers, bank or card numbers, or loan account details. We do not need them to answer a question. Our privacy policy explains how messages and site data are handled.
