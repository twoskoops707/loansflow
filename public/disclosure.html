Updated November 11, 2026

Affiliate links (business loans and business banking)

Some business-loan pages (SBA, term loans, lines of credit and equipment financing) may include a “Compare lenders” box with links to third-party companies. If you click one of these links and apply or sign up, we may earn a commission from that company at no extra cost to you. These links are marked sponsored, and we do not accept payment to change a rating, rate figure or recommendation. We are not a lender and do not make credit decisions.

Mortgages: no affiliate links

Our mortgage, refinance and home-affordability pages contain no affiliate or referral links of any kind. We do not accept per-lead or per-loan payments for mortgage referrals. Those pages are supported by display advertising only.
